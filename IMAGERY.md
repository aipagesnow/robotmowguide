# IMAGERY.md — photo direction for RobotMowGuide

Read with STYLE.md, IMAGES.md, AUDIENCE.md, and MARK-IMAGINE.md. This file is the photo direction a builder follows. Diagrams stay on the cyanotype rules in STYLE.md. The slot list and the review gate stay in IMAGES.md.

## Light
Imagery is bright, natural daylight in US home yards.

- Colour feel: fresh greens, open sky, warm daylight. Grass reads green. Sky reads blue. Sunlight is warm, not amber-filtered and not cold blue-grey.
- Use morning or midday sun. Shadows are short enough that a fence, a walk, and a slope still read.
- Never use a dark, moody, dusk, or night hero.
- Never put a heavy dark overlay on a photo. Do not dim a picture so type can sit on it. Type sits on the page.

## Subjects to use
- Green lawns that look mowed and slightly imperfect.
- Real yards: a front yard, a back yard, or both. Include slopes, trees, fences, walks, and driveways when the page is about those.
- US home cues: siding or brick, a sidewalk, a curb, a wooden fence, a curbside mailbox with no name and no number. Southern pages may show warm-season grass. Northern pages may show taller cool-season grass.
- Mowers working on the lawn: cutting, turning, or docked at the edge of the grass in daylight.
- Product-style shots are generic, unbranded, and resemble no real model. They are never a packshot. Show that kind of mower on a light ground, in daylight, with nothing else in the frame.

## Subjects to avoid
- Cartoon robots.
- Brand-traced mowers. Do not copy a real model’s shape, colourway, logo, antenna, or wheel layout. Do not draw “inspired by” versions of a named mower.
- Fake people testing. No person with a tape, a clipboard, or a phone posed over a mower. No test lawn, no lab, no measuring rig.
- Text, logos, signs, house numbers, plates, or watermarks inside the pixels.
- Manufacturer press shots, Amazon images, and stock photos.
- European gardens, night scenes, and crushed blacks.

A slot brief in IMAGES.md may still say “no mower” for that slot. Follow the brief for that slot. New briefs follow this file.

## Crops
- Hero: 16:9, with a 4:5 mobile crop. Keep the upper area bright sky or bright lawn so the H1 stays readable at 390 px without a dark band. Do not let the photo push the first answer off the first screen.
- Cards: 4:3. One yard situation in the frame (a bank, trees, beds, or a side passage). No caption under the photo.
- In-article: the same daylight. Crop tight on the yard feature the section is about. Do not mat the photo with a dark border.

## Alt text
- One sentence. Name the yard, the light, and the feature a buyer would recognize.
- Do not name a brand or a model in photo alt text. Do not say the picture is a test or the reader’s yard.
- Mood photos still get accurate alt. Diagrams get a `<title>` and a `<desc>` that match the numbers on the page.
- No words baked into the image. Alt text carries the description.

## Until a photo is approved
Do not render the photo. Do not leave an empty frame. Use the light page background in STYLE.md (Paper, Sheet, or a very light sky or lawn tint).
