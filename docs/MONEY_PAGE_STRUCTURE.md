# Money Page Structure for High Conversion

Based on affiliate SEO best practices (Affilorama and others, 2026):

## Recommended Skeleton (top to bottom)
1. **Affiliate disclosure** (near top, clear and plain language)
2. **Quick verdict / summary box** (Best Overall, Best Value, Best for Small Lawns, etc. — within first scroll)
3. **Comparison table** (key specs, badges, CTAs)
4. **Who it’s for / who it’s not for**
5. **Detailed mini-reviews or sections** with honest pros/cons
6. **Testing notes or methodology** (builds E-E-A-T)
7. **Pricing and where to buy**
8. **FAQs** (captures long-tail, reassures before click)
9. **Multiple clear CTAs** (contextual, not spammy)

## Tips
- Opinionated recommendations convert better than purely neutral lists.
- Place strong CTAs after verdict, in tables, and after pros.
- Use schema (Review, Product, FAQ).
- Mobile-first tables and buttons.
- Original images throughout.

This structure ranks better (useful, low bounce) and converts better (answers objections in order).
