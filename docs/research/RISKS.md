# RobotMowGuide: risks (format, ranking, compliance, growth)

Each risk has a likelihood (L/M/H), an impact (L/M/H), the mitigation, and the matching recommendation in `RECOMMENDATIONS.md`. Evidence is in `FORMAT-RESEARCH.md`.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Rec # |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Read as a "derivative layer" spec-summary site** in core updates (no testing, specs from manufacturer pages, rewritten consensus). The Dec 2025 and May 2026 analyses show this type losing hardest. | H | H | Original analysis per page (key numbers, owner tallies, manufacturer Q&A), tools, a researched-how box. Don't ship pages without at least one original artifact. | 1, 2, 6, 26 |
| R2 | **AI content farm perception / scaled content abuse**: AI-drafted rounds, uniform templates, AI imagery, organization byline. Spam reports can now trigger manual actions (Apr 2026). | M | H | Human fact-check gate (existing), AI-use disclosure, shape rotation, varied structures, real editor, IPTC on AI images, no auto-generated page sets. | 5, 6, 18, 33 |
| R3 | **Portfolio footprint**: Aivora runs several sites. "Creating multiple sites with the intent of hiding the scaled nature" and link exchanges are spam. | M | H | Distinct design, voice and author setup per site; no cross-links or shared boilerplate; no shared link-building networks. | 33 |
| R4 | **Fabricated or inflated authority**: a fake persona, AI headshot, "we tested", or first-person use claims from owner reports. This breaks Google policy and the FTC (16 CFR 465 penalties). | L (the plan bans it) | H | Keep the bans; attribute every owner claim with a link; a real person or an honest organization byline. | 5 |
| R5 | **Amazon Associates termination**: cached or paraphrased prices, star ratings, scraped images, a redirect/cloaked link route added "for tracking", affiliate links in email, misleading availability on discontinued models. | M | H | Compliance checklist additions; direct links; GA4 beacon tracking; remove buttons on dead ASINs; ship-scan regexes for `$`, "stars", "rating", `/go/`. | 10, 17, 31 |
| R6 | **Account suspension for no sales**: Amazon closes accounts without qualifying sales within 180 days of signup, and the Creators API needs sales to unlock. | M (depends on the account's age; holdlab-20 already exists) | M | Check holdlab-20's sales status; don't depend on API data; drive early clicks via the finder and quick-picks. | 4, 10 |
| R7 | **Launch timing vs the season**: if launch slips past ~Jan 2027, pages won't have ranked by the Mar–May peak, and a March core update could reshuffle a brand-new site. | M | H | Launch by Nov–Dec 2026; Feb refresh; no structural changes during peak. | 7 |
| R8 | **AI Overviews absorb the clicks** (−58% CTR for top results when an AIO shows; Pew 8% vs 15%). Answer-led pages get cited but clicked less. | H | M | Tools and decision steps AI can't complete; CTAs near answers; track GSC gen-AI and Bing AI Performance; the citation itself brings brand searches. | 1, 19, 22 |
| R9 | **FAQ/schema expectations**: build time spent on FAQPage or a Review/Product rich-result hope that won't pay off. | H (if unchanged) | L | Drop FAQPage; keep the Review/Rating ban; Article / Breadcrumb / Org / WebSite only. | 3 |
| R10 | **Thin or duplicate model pages**: per-SKU variants, every vs permutation, or near-identical templated reviews (thin affiliation / doorway). | M | M | One page per family; vs only with real demand; vary structures. | 16 |
| R11 | **Stale facts in a fast-moving market**: model turnover, FCC rule changes, subscription and 4G pricing changes, ASIN swaps. A wrong spec breaks trust and Amazon's misleading-claims rule. | H | M | 90-day re-check (existing); status field; dated "what changed"; FCC page re-verified on each change. | 15, 17 |
| R12 | **FCC Covered List effects**: new foreign-made models can't get authorization, so the US lineup may freeze or shrink; pages on models that can't be imported mislead. | M | M | Status labels with dates; link the explainer; avoid recommending models without a valid FCC ID. Facts from FCC-RULE.md (other worker). | 8, 17 |
| R13 | **Over-monetized look**: too many buttons, a sticky bar on list pages. Google's Discover surveys ask whether content "feels too focused on selling". | M | M | CTA budget per page type; a sticky bar only on reviews and only after testing. | 4, 13 |
| R14 | **Wrong-market traffic**: the US .com showing in the UK/CA index without matching models (May 2026 analysis: −55–59% for such sites). | L–M | L–M | Clearly US content; OneLink only for clicks; no hreflang without localized pages. | — |
| R15 | **Accessibility complaints or poor UX**: contrast of the Flag-pink CTA, sticky bars obscuring focus, an inaccessible finder. ADA web suits are common in the US. | L–M | M | Build to WCAG 2.2 AA from round 1; round 5 audit. | 32 |
| R16 | **Finder creates doorway/crawl waste**: indexable result URLs for every input combination. | M (if built naively) | M | No result routes; client state, or `noindex` + canonical to the tool page. | 1 |
| R17 | **Link-building shortcuts**: buying links, guest-post swaps, Reddit astroturfing, undisclosed self-promotion. This is link spam, can get Reddit bans, and FTC insider-review rules apply. | L–M | H | Only earned-link tactics: data, tools, PR, disclosed participation. | 8, 27 |
| R18 | **Unreliable evidence base**: much 2026 "core update analysis" is recycled (the repeated 71% figure). Decisions built on it may be wrong. | M | L–M | The plan relies on primary sources; practitioner data used only directionally. Re-check after each core update with your own GSC data. | — |
| R19 | **Sandbox-like slow start** demotivates: Ahrefs shows 1.74% of new pages reach the top 10 in a year. | H | M | Set expectations: long-tail first, Bing/Copilot early, head terms in 12+ months; judge success on impressions, finder use and CTR first. | 7 |
| R20 | **Over-investing in llms.txt / "AI SEO" gimmicks**: Google ignores them and 97% get zero requests. | M | L | Keep it minimal; spend the time on tools and original data. | 21 |
