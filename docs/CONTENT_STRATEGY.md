# Content Strategy for Success

## Mix
- Majority informational / supporting content (guides, how-tos, maintenance) to build topical authority and E-E-A-T.
- Commercial money pages (pillar roundup, comparisons, individual reviews) for revenue.
- Rough target: strong topical coverage first, then deepen money pages.

## Cluster Approach
- Pillar: Best Robotic Lawn Mowers
- Clusters: by lawn size, by terrain (slopes, complex yards), setup guides, vs traditional mowers, brand comparisons, accessories.

## Keywords Focus
- Buyer intent: best robotic lawn mower, Segway Navimow vs Mammotion, best for slopes, best for large lawns, etc.
- Supporting: how to set up, maintenance, is it worth it, etc.

## Conversion Focus
- High AOV products preferred (~3% Amazon commission on lawn/garden makes volume + AOV critical).
- Clear path from informational content to money pages via internal links.
- Trust signals: disclosures, balanced reviews, original visuals, methodology.

## Ongoing
- Update models and pricing seasonally.
- Monitor rankings and clicks; expand winning clusters.
